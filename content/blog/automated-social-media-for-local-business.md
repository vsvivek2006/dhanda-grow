## The Social Media Problem Local Businesses Face

Let's be honest about the core issue. Social media is a time drain. Taking a photo, writing a caption, designing a graphic, picking the right hashtags, and posting at the optimal time—that's easily an hour per post. Multiply that by five or six posts a week, and you're looking at a part-time job you never asked for.

As a result, local businesses tend to fall into one of two traps:

1. **Posting sporadically.** A post when you remember, then silence for two weeks. This kills your reach because social platforms reward consistency.
2. **Posting everything in a rush.** You batch five posts on Sunday night, but the designs look generic and the captions don't reflect your brand voice.

Both approaches hurt your visibility. And in 2026, with more businesses fighting for attention on local feeds, that's a problem you can't afford.
