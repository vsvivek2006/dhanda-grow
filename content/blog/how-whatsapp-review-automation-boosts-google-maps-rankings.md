## The Power of WhatsApp in Local Indian Commerce

In India, WhatsApp is not just a messaging app—it is the digital operating system for daily communication. When a customer receives an email asking for a Google review, the open rate is rarely above 15%. When you send a personalized, polite WhatsApp message thanking them for their visit and providing a direct 1-tap link to your Google Maps listing, the response rate explodes.

### Why Review Velocity Impacts Google Maps Ranking

Google Maps algorithms heavily weigh three review signals:

1. **Review Frequency (Velocity):** Consistent new ratings week after week demonstrate active operations.
2. **Review Recency:** Fresh reviews posted in the last 30 days carry more ranking power.
3. **Keyword Rich Feedback:** When satisfied customers mention specific dishes, services, or locations in their reviews, Google indexes those terms for local searches.
